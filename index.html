ABOUT US

India's Premium Makhana Brand

Shakti Sudha is a food processing company established in 2006 in technical collaboration with National Research Centre for Makhana (ICAR) and guided by reputed institutions of India.

Our Vision

To spread awareness about the nutritional benefits of makhana worldwide.

Our Mission

Deliver healthy and premium makhana products with innovation and quality.

Business Strategy

Focus on global expansion and innovative healthy snack experiences.

Objectives

Support farmers while creating nutritious and delicious products for everyone.
